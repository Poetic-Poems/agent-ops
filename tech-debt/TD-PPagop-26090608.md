Noticed while approving #1197 (issue #610, D22 — actor and model scorecards).

## The gap

#1197 removes `counts.coordinator_verdicts` and its panel, folding only the corroboration rate (plus a new picks-landed figure) into the Co-Ordinator card's own `measure`. Three surfaces that existed on `main` now render nowhere and are computed nowhere:

- **`by_band`** — requirement 3x's per-band rejection tally (issues #322, #345): which band the fleet's verdicts keep getting wrong, and the unaccounted item count behind it. Its stated purpose was that "a rejection rate concentrated in `issues` is a different failure, and a different fix, from one concentrated in `merge-conflicts`" — the new card's per-model rate cannot answer that.
- **`fallbacks` / `retries`** — what a rejection actually cost: retry engagements spent, and items the Script had to pick itself. `agent-cycle.sh` still writes `selected_by: "script-fallback"` (lines 2412, 2776) for exactly this reader; after #1197 the field has no reader in the repository.
- **`last_rejection`** — the newest contradiction with its unaccounted refs, its own stated reason and what became of the cycle (recovered by retry / by the Script's pick / stood down).

## Why it matters

This was in scope for #1197: acceptance criterion 4 on #610 asks only that the *rate* be folded in and the old panel not left rendering beside it, so dropping the rest was a legitimate reading and the Implementer flagged it in the pull-request body. But the drop is unrecorded anywhere durable, and two of the three figures are the ones that name a fix rather than a symptom — a rate says "the model is wrong", the band tally and the fallback count say which band and what it cost. Without a record, the next reader of requirement 3x will find a tally the spec describes the purpose of and no surface that shows it.

## Where

`scripts/publish-dashboard.sh` (the `actor_scorecards` block, replacing the former `coordinator_verdicts` block), `dashboard/index.html` (`scorecardsPanel`, replacing `verdictQualityPanel`), `docs/DASHBOARD-SPEC.md` ("Actor and model scorecards"), `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md` requirements 3v/3x.

## Options

1. Restore the two actionable figures as facets of the Co-Ordinator card — per-model `fallbacks`/`retries` alongside the corroboration rate, and a small band table beneath the Co-Ordinator card.
2. Restore the newest-rejection detail box too, as a third element of that card.
3. Accept the loss deliberately, and amend requirement 3x (and 3v's "Distinguishable in the log" bullet) to stop describing a surface the product no longer has.

## Default: Option 1 — carry `fallbacks`/`retries` per model into the Co-Ordinator row and render the band tally under that card: it restores the two figures that name a fix, keeps D22's one-card-per-actor shape rather than reviving a second panel beside it, and needs no new event fields since `selected_by` and `bands` are already on the record.
