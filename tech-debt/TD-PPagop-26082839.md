title: "disable_default_ttl and none_selected_recheck_hours take requirement 1d's worst-case gap, where longer is the unsafe direction"
status: open
filed: 2026-08-28
---

Requirement 1d derives its four hour-valued keys against the worst-case gap between firings, on the argument that the worst case is "the conservative answer for the four hour-valued keys". That argument holds for the two the requirement then floors at a cycle's own runtime — `claim_ttl_hours` and `abandoned_draft_after_hours` each have to outlast a quiet stretch, so longer is safer. It does not hold for the other two, where longer is the direction that hurts:

- `none_selected_recheck_hours` is the no-op short-circuit's safety valve (requirement 3b), and its documented job is to bound how long a gap in fingerprint coverage can stall the pipeline. On a `cycle_hours: "9-17"`, 15-minute installation the derivation yields **366 hours** — a fortnight in which a fingerprint bug stalls the pipeline unseen, where the 24 firings the key means are about 16 hours of that installation's wall clock.
- `disable_default_ttl` yields **61 hours** on the same installation: a `--disable` with no `--for` outlasts the weekend. On Poetic's own unrestricted 15-minute cadence it yields **1 hour**, against a documented intent of "long enough to cover an editing session" — a human quantity that may not follow the cadence in either direction.

Both would be consistent under the mean gap PR #886 adopted for the three count-valued keys (2.7 h and 16 h respectively on that installation), since each of these keys expresses a number of firings elapsed rather than a stretch that must be outlasted. Neither, though, contradicts a property requirement 1d states outright, and `disable_default_ttl` may not belong in the derivation at all: the thing it is sized against is a person's editing session, not the pipeline's throughput.

Suggested fix: decide, per key, which of the three it is — a stretch to outlast (worst-case gap, as now), a count of firings elapsed (mean gap), or a human-world duration that should leave requirement 1d's derivation altogether and go back to a plain schema default beside `enabler_recheck_hours` and `human_nudge_idle_hours`. Then move the key and say which in requirement 1d's own categorisation, which currently lists all four under one heading.
