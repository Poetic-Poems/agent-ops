`lib/handoff.sh`'s `ensure_human_reviewer` (requirement 38a) returns the same
bare `skip` for three unrelated reasons: the pull request is a draft, something
is already `CHANGES_REQUESTED`-blocking it, or — the case this item is about —
there is no legal review-request candidate left once the author is struck from
both the known-reviewers list and the `enabler_assignee` fallback. The first two
are fine to leave alone (they have their own actor and their own clock); the
third is a genuine human-visibility gap `scripts/sweep-human-visibility.sh`
reaches every cycle and can never correct — nobody is ever asked, and, because
the three cases share one return value, the sweep cannot even tell this one
apart from the other two to log a `warning` about it. It is a silent skip by
design (see `sweep-human-visibility.sh`'s header note), and stays invisible to
`scripts/gather-human-visibility-hygiene.sh` (requirement 38e,
tech-debt/TD-PPagop-26080801.md) for exactly the same reason: that gatherer
reads the sweep's `warning` events back out of the log union, and this case
never produces one.

Fixing it properly needs `ensure_human_reviewer` to distinguish "no candidate"
from its other two `skip` reasons in its return value — `skip\tno-candidate` or
similar — which is a change to a function with three call sites
(`sweep-human-visibility.sh`, the Reviewer's handoff, and the Enabler's
`complete_handoff` in `agent-cycle.sh`) and its own extensive regression
coverage (`test/handoff.test.sh`). That is real, but separate, work: deferred
here rather than folded into TD-PPagop-26080801.md's fix, to keep that PR's
diff to the log-reading half of the problem it actually claimed.

Suggested fix: give `ensure_human_reviewer` a distinguishable "no candidate"
return, have the sweep log a `warning` for it (or a dedicated event), and
extend `scripts/gather-human-visibility-hygiene.sh`'s candidate rule to cover
it — the live-reverification the gatherer already does (re-checking the pull
request is still open and still has no candidate) generalises to this case
without much new machinery once the signal exists to read.
