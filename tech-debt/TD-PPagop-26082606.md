Noticed while reviewing PR #817 (issue #604), which is where the
mirror-rebuild verdict was added. Not a defect in that pull request: #604's
acceptance criterion 3 names the heartbeat as the channel and does not name
the dashboard, and the heartbeat is exactly what shipped. This records the
gap that scope leaves between "the fleet can see it" and "a human can see
it".

## What

`scripts/state-sync.sh`'s push stamps `heartbeat.json` with a `mirror` field
(`lib/mirror-integrity.sh`'s `mirror_rebuild_verdict`): `null` until this
node has had to discard and rebuild its state-sync mirror, else
`{status: "rebuilt", count, last_rebuilt_at}`. It sits alongside `compose`,
`image`, `switch` and `stage_health` in the same object, and it replicates
the same way — a peer's fetch materialises the whole heartbeat, so the fact
does reach every node in the fleet.

What it does not reach is the page anyone actually reads. Each of the other
four verdicts is carried out of the heartbeat into `data.js` by
`scripts/publish-dashboard.sh` — for this node at its `--argjson` block
(`scripts/publish-dashboard.sh:1024`, `:1766`) and for a peer in the
`for hb in "$peers_dir"/*/heartbeat.json` loop (`:1789` for `compose`,
`:1806` for `stage_health`) — and rendered as a per-node badge by
`dashboard/index.html` (`switchBadge` at `:897`, compose at `:1185`, image
at `:1217`, stage health at `:1351`). `mirror` is carried by neither and
rendered by neither.

So today a human learns their disk has been quietly damaging a mirror only
by running `jq` over a file in the peers directory, having first thought to
suspect it — which is close to the condition #604 was filed to end, where
the damage was real for four days and nothing said so.

## Why it matters

The count is the part with the most to say and the least chance of being
read. A single rebuild is a transient worth shrugging at; `count: 7` on one
node is a failing disk, and distinguishing those two is the stated reason
the verdict carries a count at all rather than a boolean. That distinction
is only useful to whoever sees it, and the dashboard is where node facts get
seen.

## What it would take

The same shape the other four verdicts already have, which is why this is
small rather than novel:

- `scripts/publish-dashboard.sh`: add `mirror` to the local node's row
  (`:1766`) and to the peer row's `($h.mirror // null)` mapping (beside
  `:1806`), so a heartbeat built before this field existed yields `null`
  rather than an error.
- `dashboard/index.html`: a badge on the node card, on `compose`'s
  precedent (`:1185`) — amber for a rebuild, with `count` and
  `last_rebuilt_at` in the title, and nothing at all when the verdict is
  `null`.
- `docs/DASHBOARD-SPEC.md`: the node-card requirement that enumerates which
  verdicts a card shows.
- `test/publish-dashboard.test.sh` and `test/dashboard-render.test.sh`, on
  the pattern the stage-health rows added in #774.

Worth doing together with TD-PPagop-26082605 if that one is picked up, since
both end in the same two files and the same two tests.
