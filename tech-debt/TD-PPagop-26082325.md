title: landing_eligible's unknown verdict always blames the changed-file list, even when the protected-paths list was the unevaluable part
status: open
filed: 2026-08-23
---

`landing_protected_paths_hit` (`lib/landing.sh`) returns exit 2 for three
distinct causes: bad arguments or a `gh` error, a changed-file listing that
reached `LANDING_PR_FILES_LIMIT`, and — since TD-PPagop-26082320 — a
`merge_autonomy_protected_paths` list `_landing_is_protected` cannot evaluate
against a path at all. `landing_eligible` collapses all three into one
string:

```sh
2)
  printf 'unknown:could not establish %s#%s'\''s changed-file list' "$slug" "$number"
```

The verdict is right — `unknown`, never a pass — but the explanation is
wrong for the third cause: the changed-file list was read perfectly well, and
what could not be established was the operator's own configured list. That
string is the whole diagnostic an operator gets, and the one they get most of
(agent-ops#718 held D18 arming shut for five days while the logs said only
`unknown:could not establish …'s changed-file list`), so a malformed
`merge_autonomy_protected_paths` override would send whoever debugs it to
`gh`, pagination and the `--method GET` gotcha rather than to their own
`config.json`.

The deliberate twin does better: `scripts/detect-classifier-escapes.sh` names
its own reason exactly (`the protected-path list could not be evaluated
against the merge's changed files (a non-string entry in
merge_autonomy_protected_paths)`). The two implementations are pinned
identical on the *verdict* by `test/detect-classifier-escapes.test.sh`, and
nothing pins — or now matches — the *explanation*.

**Not a correctness gap.** Every call site treats exit 2 as a refusal
whatever caused it, and the malformed-list case is unreachable through a
schema-validated `config.json` (`config.schema.json` constrains every entry
to a non-empty string); `scripts/detect-classifier-escapes.sh`'s own
`--config` read is the one ungated path, and that script already explains
itself properly. This is a diagnosability defect only.

**Suggested fix.** Give `landing_protected_paths_hit` a way to say which
cause it hit — a fourth exit code, or a reason on stderr the caller can
carry — and have `landing_eligible` print the matching `unknown:` reason, the
way the detector already does. Requirement 8d (and acceptance check 8t, which
pins the current wording) in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md` move with
it, as does `test/landing.test.sh`'s pinned `unknown:could not establish
acme/widgets#12's changed-file list` assertion.

Noticed while reviewing PR #733 (TD-PPagop-26082320), which introduced the
third cause.
