Issue #591's audit says of the count-valued keys, under section C: "Where a derivation raises a retained volume, state the resulting mirror size — that is D14's trade-off rule applying to this change." Requirement 1d (PR #886) makes the derivation but not the statement.

On Poetic's 15-minute installation the derivation raises `cycles_retained` 200 → 800 (the mirror force-pushed to `state_repo` after every cycle), `state_local_cycles_retained` 1000 → 4000 and `state_local_streams_retained` 50 → 200 — all fourfold. Requirement 1d, the CHANGELOG entry and the README rows each state that the *window* is preserved, which is the intent rather than the cost. The `state_local_streams_retained` raise is the one worth a number: scripts/state-sync.sh's own header calls a single Reviewer stream "megabytes" against a cycle directory's "handful of kilobytes", which is why that key is bounded so far below the record count in the first place — quadrupling it quadruples the largest thing on the node's disk, and nothing in #886 says by how much in absolute terms.

Suggested fix: measure a live node's `state_dir` and mirror, and record the resulting sizes at the shipped cadence in requirement 1d (or in the extended notes for the three keys), so the next cadence change has a baseline to reason from rather than only a ratio.

Noticed while reviewing https://github.com/Poetic-Poems/agent-ops/pull/886; raised at https://github.com/Poetic-Poems/agent-ops/pull/886#issuecomment-5447070998.
