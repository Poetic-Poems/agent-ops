TD-PPagop-26082830 asked for the resulting size of the three retained volumes requirement 1d raises, and PR #886 answered it with an estimate: `state_local_streams_retained`'s 200 retained cycles is put at "low hundreds of megabytes on a busy repository", with `cycles_retained` and `state_local_cycles_retained` at tens of megabytes each. The requirement says so itself — "not a measurement of any live node's actual `state_dir`, which is what would replace this estimate with a real baseline".

That is the right thing to have written and the wrong thing to leave untracked. The estimate is derived from `scripts/state-sync.sh`'s own prose ("megabytes" for one Reviewer stream, "a handful of kilobytes" for a cycle directory without its derived files), not from a node, and the one check a raise here could actually trip — `min_free_workspace_bytes`' 2 GiB pre-clone floor — is judged clear on that basis alone. A cadence change, or a repository whose stage streams run larger than this fleet's, would have nothing better than the same prose to reason from.

Suggested fix: measure `state_dir` and the state-repository mirror on a live node at the shipped cadence — total size, and the per-cycle-directory size with and without the derived files — and record the figures in requirement 1d's own extended notes for the three keys, replacing the estimate. `scripts/publish-dashboard.sh` and `scripts/doctor.sh` already read `state_dir`, so a periodic figure is within reach if a one-off measurement proves too perishable to be worth recording.

Noticed while reviewing https://github.com/Poetic-Poems/agent-ops/pull/886, in which TD-PPagop-26082830 was resolved by the estimate this record is about.
