TD-PPagop-26082305 (PR #805) stops the Refiner *manufacturing* the deadlock
agent-ops#670 described: an item it judges already adequately specified is now
`refined` (re-affirmation, requirement 39c) rather than a `needs-refinement`
decline whose block projects the very label its `unblock_condition` names.
That is a fix to the shape. The two original instances are still standing.

## What is still true on 2026-08-26

Both issues remain **open** and still carry `blocked` and
`blocked:needs-refinement`:

- **#597** — `blocked` + `blocked:needs-refinement` applied
  2026-08-21T12:45:10Z; the hand-flag `needs-refinement` label that block was
  raised over was removed 2026-08-21T19:25:18Z.
- **#598** — the same pair applied 2026-08-21T12:45:07Z; its hand-flag
  `needs-refinement` removed 2026-08-22T00:11:03Z.

Both escalations asking for exactly this cleanup — #660 (for #597) and #666
(for #598) — are closed, and agent-ops#670's own "Out of scope" section records
the retroactive cleanup as "already done by hand". The label history says
otherwise: five days after the hand-flag came off, the block projection is
still on both issues.

## Why it matters

Requirement 39a excludes a blocked item from Refiner candidacy, and the
selection walk excludes it too. So for as long as the projection stands, the
re-affirmation path PR #805 adds is unreachable for #597 and #598 — the fix
prevents new instances of the deadlock without releasing the two that
prompted it. Nothing in the pipeline is expected to notice: from its side
these are two ordinary blocked items.

Two possibilities, and which one holds decides the fix:

1. **The block is still open in the shared log.** Then the labels are an
   accurate projection and what needs clearing is the block itself — which,
   per requirement 34c, is a human's line appended to `state_dir/log.jsonl`,
   or an Enabler `unblocked` verdict.
2. **The block cleared and the projection did not come off.** Then
   `label_own_stale_applications`' removal path did not run, or ran and
   failed, for these two — and that is a defect in the projection's own
   cleanup worth pinning with a test, not just a hand cleanup.

Distinguishing them needs a read of the fleet's `log.jsonl`, which is not
available from a review clone; that read is the first step of whatever fix
this item gets.

## Suggested fix

Read the shared log for open `needs-refinement` blocks naming
`Poetic-Poems/agent-ops` items `597` and `598`. If they are open, clear them by
the requirement 34c interface and let the projection come off on its own,
confirming it does. If they are already closed, treat the standing labels as
the bug: find why the stale-removal path left them, fix it, and pin it.

Either way, finish by confirming both issues re-enter the Refiner's candidate
set and that a re-affirmation (requirement 39c) is what they get — which is
the end-to-end verification of agent-ops#670 Part 2 that PR #805's unit-level
replay could not perform against live state.

Noticed while reviewing https://github.com/Poetic-Poems/agent-ops/pull/805.

## Determined 2026-08-26: possibility 2, and it is wider than two items

The read this record said it needed — the fleet's `log.jsonl` — was done on
the workstation, over `ockham-container`'s own state directory plus the three
peer trees `state-sync.sh fetch` materialises under `.agent-ops-peers`
(`poetic-1`, `poetic-2`, `ockham-2`). Possibility 2 holds: the blocks are
closed and the projection did not come off. Branch 1 of the suggested fix
above is therefore closed out — no human `log.jsonl` line under requirement
34c is needed for either issue.

**Both blocks cleared, and are recorded as cleared.** #597: `unblocked`,
`by: label-removed`, at 2026-08-21T19:54:24Z (`ockham-container`) and
2026-08-21T20:21:54Z (`poetic-1`). #598: the same pair at
2026-08-22T00:36:54Z and 2026-08-22T01:11:23Z. Each follows the hand-flag
removal this record's opening section dates.

**Seven issues are stuck, not two.** Of the 14 open `Poetic-Poems/agent-ops`
issues carrying `blocked:needs-refinement` on 2026-08-26, six have a block
still open in the log and are projected correctly (#595, #596, #599, #600,
#601, #603). Seven do not: **#597, #598, #602, #604, #606, #613, #640** —
#597 and #598 cleared `by: label-removed`, the other five `by: enabler`. A
fifteenth, #810, carries the pair with no block event in any node's log at
all; it is newer than the rest and is left out of the count here rather than
guessed at.

The pipeline's own record says the projection is what excludes them, in as
many words: every `issues-excluded` event since names all seven with the
reason `blocked-label` — e.g. `2026-08-25T06:24:37Z ockham-container 20
issue(s) excluded: … #597 (blocked-label), #598 (blocked-label), #602
(blocked-label), … #640 (blocked-label)`.

**Why none of the seven can self-heal.** Not one carries an
`own-label-action` for `blocked` or `blocked:needs-refinement` in any node's
log; the only label actions recorded against them are for the
`needs-refinement` hand flag. The pair was applied on 2026-08-21T12:45 by
`scripts/sweep-legacy-refinement-assignees.sh`, which records no event of its
own — the consequence `refinement_blocked_label_targets`' own comment already
names ("the sweep has no event of its own to record which of `added`/
`present` actually happened … a legacy block's `blocked_label` field can
never be filled the way a fresh block's is"). Requirement 38b's every-cycle
reconciliation (`lib/candidate-gather.sh`, via
`refinement_blocked_label_stale`) then keys strictly on a logged
`own-label-action add`: "a logged `own-label-action add` with no later
`remove` is proof enough on its own that a removal is ours to retry". No
add, no retry. So the backstop written for exactly this
permanently-stuck-hold class is structurally blind to every label that sweep
applied — not for these seven only, but for any item it ever touched.

**And for the `by: label-removed` half, nothing even tried.**
`release_refinement_label` is called from three sites, all of them in
`lib/enabler.sh`. The hand-flag clear in `lib/candidate-gather.sh` logs its
`unblocked` and attempts no blocked-label removal at all — which is the whole
of what happened to #597 and #598.

**#602 shows a third face of it.** Its block cleared at 2026-08-21T07:52:09Z
(`unblocked`, `by: enabler`) and the sweep projected a label onto it at
12:45 — five hours after the block it was projecting had already gone.

### What is left to fix

1. The one-off cleanup: seven issues, not two, and both labels on each. The
   generic `blocked` keeps its documented over-hold (`blocked` is a human's
   own control, so the code will not remove it on a legacy block), so a hand
   removal has to take the pair.
2. The defect, which is what makes this more than a cleanup: either the sweep
   records its own applications so `refinement_blocked_label_stale` can see
   them, or the reconciliation stops requiring a logged add for the compound
   `blocked:<reason>` label — which, unlike `blocked`, no human ever applies
   by hand, as `refinement_blocked_label_targets` already argues in the
   course of treating it as unconditionally the sweep's own. Whichever, pin
   it with a test.
3. The sweep should not project onto a block that has already cleared
   (#602).

The end-to-end confirmation this record already asks for is unchanged: that
the affected issues re-enter the Refiner's candidate set and that a
re-affirmation (requirement 39c) is what they get.
