TD-PPagop-26082305 (PR #805) stops the Refiner *manufacturing* the deadlock
agent-ops#670 described: an item it judges already adequately specified is now
`refined` (re-affirmation, requirement 39c) rather than a `needs-refinement`
decline whose block projects the very label its `unblock_condition` names.
That is a fix to the shape. The two original instances are still standing.

## What is still true on 2026-08-26

Both issues remain **open** and still carry `blocked` and
`blocked:needs-refinement`:

- **#597** — `blocked` + `blocked:needs-refinement` applied
  2026-08-21T12:45:10Z; the hand-flag `needs-refinement` label that block was
  raised over was removed 2026-08-21T19:25:18Z.
- **#598** — the same pair applied 2026-08-21T12:45:07Z; its hand-flag
  `needs-refinement` removed 2026-08-22T00:11:03Z.

Both escalations asking for exactly this cleanup — #660 (for #597) and #666
(for #598) — are closed, and agent-ops#670's own "Out of scope" section records
the retroactive cleanup as "already done by hand". The label history says
otherwise: five days after the hand-flag came off, the block projection is
still on both issues.

## Why it matters

Requirement 39a excludes a blocked item from Refiner candidacy, and the
selection walk excludes it too. So for as long as the projection stands, the
re-affirmation path PR #805 adds is unreachable for #597 and #598 — the fix
prevents new instances of the deadlock without releasing the two that
prompted it. Nothing in the pipeline is expected to notice: from its side
these are two ordinary blocked items.

Two possibilities, and which one holds decides the fix:

1. **The block is still open in the shared log.** Then the labels are an
   accurate projection and what needs clearing is the block itself — which,
   per requirement 34c, is a human's line appended to `state_dir/log.jsonl`,
   or an Enabler `unblocked` verdict.
2. **The block cleared and the projection did not come off.** Then
   `label_own_stale_applications`' removal path did not run, or ran and
   failed, for these two — and that is a defect in the projection's own
   cleanup worth pinning with a test, not just a hand cleanup.

Distinguishing them needs a read of the fleet's `log.jsonl`, which is not
available from a review clone; that read is the first step of whatever fix
this item gets.

## Suggested fix

Read the shared log for open `needs-refinement` blocks naming
`Poetic-Poems/agent-ops` items `597` and `598`. If they are open, clear them by
the requirement 34c interface and let the projection come off on its own,
confirming it does. If they are already closed, treat the standing labels as
the bug: find why the stale-removal path left them, fix it, and pin it.

Either way, finish by confirming both issues re-enter the Refiner's candidate
set and that a re-affirmation (requirement 39c) is what they get — which is
the end-to-end verification of agent-ops#670 Part 2 that PR #805's unit-level
replay could not perform against live state.
