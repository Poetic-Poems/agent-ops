**Severity:** Low

No `SECURITY.md` exists (no private vulnerability-disclosure route); no
`CONTRIBUTING.md` exists (`CLAUDE.md` documents branch/PR conventions but
is written for an operating AI agent, not a human contributor); no
`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` or `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` exist. The
current `LICENCE` is MIT, but `docs/ROADMAP.md` decision D5 states the
eventual product licence is an open question ("Source-available
(BSL/FSL-family) … exact licence is an open question with a Phase 1
decide-by gate") with nothing flagging that to a reader today.

These are all governance artefacts a project with one operator and no
external contributors has had no occasion to need yet.

**Ongoing cost:** low today given the single-operator audience. A security
researcher who independently found TD-PPagop-26082407 would have no
private channel and might default to a public issue. The other three gaps
will matter once `docs/ROADMAP.md`'s productisation work brings in
external contributors or customers.

**Suggested fix:** add a short `SECURITY.md` naming a private disclosure
contact (the only one of these four with real urgency, given
TD-PPagop-26082407); a brief note flagging the pending licence decision; a
`CONTRIBUTING.md` pointing at `CLAUDE.md`'s existing conventions; and
issue/PR templates surfacing the `Priority` field and the Conventional
Commits title requirement.
