Requirement 17g (`item_text_fault`, `lib/candidate-select.sh`, agent-ops#821)
checks a trimmed Co-Ordinator candidate's `acceptance` — its backtick-quoted
specifics only — against the item's own live text before the candidate is
claimed. It does **not** check `context` at all, even though the #815
incident that motivated #821 was, in its most visible form, a fabricated
`context` section (a `## Why it matters` paragraph invented wholesale).

An earlier shape of PR #825 did check `context`: every paragraph of at least
80 normalized characters had to trace to the live item text or the item's
recorded refinement `spec`. Two independent Reviewer rounds blocked that PR
on the same finding, reproduced against the PR's own work order: `context` is
required by `prompts/coordinator.md`'s work-order schema to carry the
Co-Ordinator's own framing — "file paths, related conventions found while
evaluating, why the item is unblocked and in scope" — *alongside* the entry's
verbatim paste, and nothing in the schema marks where the paste ends and the
framing begins. A check that faults every paragraph it cannot trace therefore
faults that mandated framing by construction, on ordinary honest work orders,
at the fit rungs the fleet normally runs at (issue #821's own figures: 90% of
`coordinator-input-fitted` events land at rungs 7–8).

The owner decision on agent-ops#830 (recorded on #821 itself) chose to drop
the `context` check entirely rather than narrow it, because narrowing it
needs a work-order schema change — a marker for where the verbatim paste ends
and the Co-Ordinator's own framing begins (e.g. splitting `context` into a
pasted field and a notes field, or requiring the paste to be
fenced/blockquoted) — which is a larger, separate change spanning
`prompts/coordinator.md`, `lib/candidate-select.sh`, requirement 17g,
requirement 20's verbatim-paste rule, and `test/item-text-fabrication.test.sh`
together, and depends on the cheapest model in the fleet honouring a new
convention.

Issue #769 already asks whether the Co-Ordinator should be pasting `context`
at all (framed as model-sizing and cost) — the schema question this gap
depends on. Revisit `context` fabrication detection when #769 resolves, or
sooner if a `context` fabrication incident like #815's is observed again
despite the `acceptance` check and `item_text_supply`'s unconditional
live-text append.

Noticed while reworking PR #825 to agent-ops#830's decided shape.
