Noticed while reviewing PR #864 (TD-PPagop-26082502).

TD-PPagop-26082502 fixed the Autonomous landings panel's refusal grouping by
giving every colon-carrying `_landing_refuse` reason in `_landing_stage_attempt`
(`lib/landing.sh`) a stable class word ahead of its first `:` —
`approver-review-unreadable:`, `arm-failed:`, `dequeued-manual:` and the rest —
so `byReason` (`dashboard/index.html`), which groups on `reason.split(":")[0]`,
keys on the gate that refused rather than on the scheme colon of an embedded
`$pr_url`. That fix is correct for every call site that exists today.

What it does not do is make the invariant enforceable. The class lives *inside*
the human-readable reason string, so the contract between producer and consumer
is prose in three places — `lib/landing.sh`'s own header, requirement 33 and
`docs/DASHBOARD-SPEC.md`'s refusal-grouping note — and nothing mechanical holds
a new call site to it. `test/landing-wiring.test.sh` pins the class of two
specific refusals; a twelfth gate added next quarter with a sentence-form reason
embedding `$pr_url` reintroduces the exact defect TD-PPagop-26082502 was filed
for, with no failing test, no lint rule and no dashboard symptom short of an
operator noticing the singleton groups themselves.

## Why it matters

This is the same failure mode the fleet already solved structurally elsewhere.
`stand-down` events carry their three-way distinction as a `cause` field
precisely "so a reader (the dashboard included) does not have to re-parse the
reason text" (requirement 39, issue #245). `landing-refused` still makes its
reader parse prose, which is why the defect was possible at all and why it will
be possible again — the prose can drift out of the shape the consumer needs
without either side changing.

The cost is quiet by construction: the grouping degrades into singleton
"groups", which is exactly what a genuinely one-off refusal looks like, so the
signal the panel exists to give ("two landings beside forty refusals is a
classifier holding the line") is lost without anything reporting a fault.

## Suggested fix

Mint the class out of band, as TD-PPagop-26082502's own "cleaner fix" note
proposed and scoped out: give `_landing_refuse` a CLASS parameter, log it as its
own `class` field on the `landing-refused` event (requirement 33's schema), and
have `byReason` read that field, falling back to the split-on-first-`:` rule
only for events predating the field. Two things then become possible that are
not possible today: the class list is a closed set a test can enumerate against
the call sites, and a refusal logged with no class at all is a detectable
defect rather than an invisible one.
