**Severity:** Low

`.github/workflows/codeql.yml` explicitly scans only the `actions`
language (GitHub Actions workflow YAML), because CodeQL has no Shell or
Perl support. The ~27,000 lines of `lib/`, `scripts/`, and the two cycle
entry points have no automated security-pattern scanning beyond
shellcheck's style/correctness checks, which are not a security scanner —
they catch quoting and portability bugs, not injection-shaped patterns as
a category.

This is a genuine tooling gap in the shell/Perl ecosystem rather than an
oversight: CodeQL was likely adopted for its GitHub-native integration
without anyone separately sourcing a shell-aware alternative.

**Ongoing cost:** low today given the codebase's otherwise strong quoting
discipline (this review's own targeted search found nothing exploitable
outside TD-PPagop-26082407), but a security-relevant shell pattern
introduced in the future would not be caught by any automated scanner.

**Suggested fix:** evaluate a shell/Perl-aware SAST tool (e.g. Semgrep with
a community shell ruleset) against this codebase's actual style as a spike
first — false-positive rate on ~250 scripts of this specific idiom is
unknown — and add it to CI as non-blocking/report-only initially if the
signal-to-noise ratio looks workable, only promoting to a required check
after an observation period.
