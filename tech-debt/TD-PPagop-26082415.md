**Severity:** Medium

README.md has 89 headings across 2,381 lines and already relies on
internal jump-navigation (linking to its own anchors throughout), and
`docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md` is 17,320 lines — yet neither, nor
any other document under `docs/`, has a table of contents. This repository
already has a precedent for a generated, CI-checked documentation region
(`scripts/render-config-table.sh`, checked via `.github/workflows/config-table.yml`)
that a ToC generator should follow rather than a hand-written one that will
drift.

Both documents grew heading-by-heading over 42 days of fast iteration;
nothing ever prompted a dedicated pass to add navigation aids once they
crossed a size where one would help.

**Ongoing cost:** a reader has no way to see either document's shape
without scrolling or grepping, despite the documents' own extensive
internal cross-referencing being evidence the authors already know
precisely which sections matter enough to jump to.

**Suggested fix:** write a small script, in the spirit of
`scripts/render-config-table.sh`, that extracts `##`/`###` headings and
renders a ToC block between markers in both documents, checked in CI the
same way the config tables already are.
