TD-PPagop-26081301 converted the sites the 2026-08-12 argv-cap outage bit at,
plus the bounded-today ones around them; TD-PPagop-26081401 converted the seven
residual sites that record enumerated. Neither item swept the tree, and the
sites below are what a `grep -n -- --argjson` over `agent-cycle.sh`, `lib/` and
`scripts/` still finds carrying fleet state rather than the
configuration-bounded values requirement 4g permits in argv (a model name, a
config object, a prompt sha, one repo's register ids).

They are listed loudest-failure-first, on the same terms TD-PPagop-26081401
used. None is known to be near `MAX_ARG_STRLEN` (131072 bytes) today except
where noted.

- `agent-cycle.sh`'s Co-Ordinator recorded-refinement and voided accumulators
  (`--argjson e "$entry"` at the `coord_recorded_refinement_json` and
  `coord_recorded_voided_json` appends), and the two
  `{needs_refinement, voided}` builds they feed
  (`--argjson nr` / `--argjson v`, at the first-attempt and retry
  `unaccounted_items` call sites). This is the one whose failure is already
  demonstrable, and it defeats the fix TD-PPagop-26081401 just made one call
  frame downstream: past the cap the *builder* dies at `execve` (exit 126),
  its failure does not trip the enclosing `set -e` because it is a nested
  command substitution, and `unaccounted_items` is handed `""`. With an empty
  first document `input as $recorded` consumes the *eligible* array, the
  second `input` finds nothing, and the call fail-opens to `[]` — the Script
  then reports zero unaccounted items and corroborates the Co-Ordinator's
  `none-selected`, silently, exactly when there is the most recorded
  refinement to account for. Requirement 3v's contradiction record, which the
  crash-loop ladder and the dashboard read, is never written. The accumulators
  themselves are the identical shape to the Enabler and Refiner claim
  accumulators TD-PPagop-26081401 converted.
- `scripts/gather-review-feedback.sh` — the assembled review body
  (`--argjson fr "$fresh"`, `--argjson fc "$fresh_comments"`: every fresh
  review and inline comment on the pull request, verbatim), the candidate
  build (`--argjson pr "$pr"`, a whole pull-request object including its
  body) and the per-candidate append (`--argjson c "$cand"`). Genuinely
  unbounded, not merely growing — the same shape and the same reasoning that
  put `scripts/gather-merge-conflicts.sh` first on TD-PPagop-26081401's list.
  The script runs under `set -uo pipefail`, so past the cap `$cand` is empty,
  every later append fails in turn and the repo's whole `review_feedback`
  band comes out empty.
- `scripts/gather-abandoned-drafts.sh` — the candidate build
  (`--argjson pr "$pr"`) and the per-candidate append
  (`--argjson c "$cand"`), carrying `body: ($pr.body // "")`. Identical to
  the above, for the `abandoned_drafts` band.
- `scripts/gather-issues.sh` — the entry build (`--argjson comments`, a whole
  issue thread: requirement 3d/#118 pre-fetches every comment) and the array
  append (`--argjson e "$entry"`). Both are already guarded by `degrade`, so
  this one is loud on stderr and degrades that repo's whole `issues` band to
  `[]` rather than failing silently.
- `agent-cycle.sh`'s per-repo entry build (`--argjson findings`,
  `--argjson rf`, `--argjson ad`, `--argjson mc`, `--argjson rh`,
  `--argjson issues`, `--argjson td`). TD-PPagop-26081301 named `$entry` —
  one repo's whole pre-fetched sources, issue threads and open register
  included — as the least bounded value in that loop and converted the
  *append* that consumes it; the *build* that produces it still passes every
  one of those bands through argv, and each band alone can be tens of
  kilobytes.
- `lib/refinement.sh` `refiner_candidate_items` — `--argjson refinements`,
  `--argjson blocked`, `--argjson void` and `--argjson claimed`: four of the
  five aggregates requirement 4g names by name as growing with the fleet's
  history, all still in argv, with `$repos` alone on stdin. Fails into the
  function's own `2>/dev/null || printf '[]'`, so past the cap the Refiner
  silently finds no candidates at all.
- `scripts/gather-human-visibility-hygiene.sh` — the two survivor appends
  (`--argjson v "$v"`) and the final `--argjson problems`.
- `scripts/gather-unvoid-requests.sh` — the request build
  (`--argjson hit`, `--argjson items`) and the per-request append
  (`--argjson r "$req"`).
- `scripts/nudge-dependabot-rebase.sh` — the conflict accumulator
  (`--argjson c "$cand"`).

The fix is the same mechanical conversion at each: concatenate the documents
(`docs="$(printf '%s\n' "$a" "$b")"`), deliver with a here-string, bind
positionally with `input as $name` in the printed order, and leave only
configuration-bounded values in argv. Convert a site together with a test that
pins its fail-safe direction past the cap, as requirement 4g's own acceptance
discipline requires — `test/merge-conflicts.test.sh`,
`test/pr-claim-exclusion.test.sh` and their siblings are the reference. Where
a converted site currently fails loudly, keep it loud: `scripts/gather-tech-debt.sh`
and `scripts/gather-issues.sh` route the failure through `degrade` rather than
swallowing it with `2>/dev/null`, which is the direction a band whose job is
visibility should fail in.

Requirement 4g in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md` names this item as
the residue; resolving it means removing that reference along with the sites.
