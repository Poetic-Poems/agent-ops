title: "The issue-closed re-flag exemption is consumed only by an enabler-examined, so a close already acted on through a label-removed, work-gone or dependency-resolved unblock can still grant one engagement"

**What.** Requirement 35a's `issue-closed` exemption for a `needs-refinement`
re-flag (TD-PPagop-26082901, PR #939) is bounded by "no examination has
followed the escalation": `ENABLER_ELIGIBLE_JQ`'s `$examined_since_escalation`
counts `enabler-examined` events after `$escalation.ts`, and one of those is
what retires the exemption for good. That is the whole mechanism keeping the
fix inside the scope issue #934 drew for it — a close an engagement has
already consumed must not be re-spent.

But an `enabler-examined` is not the only way an item leaves its block. Four
other unblock paths exist and none of them logs one:

- `lib/candidate-gather.sh` — `by: "label-removed"` (a human takes the
  `blocked` label off), `by: "work-gone"`, `by: "dependency-resolved"`;
- `lib/candidate-select.sh` (≈ line 1100) — the bare `unblocked` event.

So an escalation the human closed weeks ago, whose ask was then fully acted
on through one of those paths, still counts as "not followed by an
examination". If that item is later re-flagged `needs-refinement` and its
latest `item-refined` still predates the escalation — likely, since the
`label-removed` path never writes one — the exemption fires on the strength
of a long-settled close. That grants an engagement with `reason:
"issue-closed"`, which is not only an examination: it is also the one
exemption to requirement 36b's thrash guard (a second refinement is
permitted) and to `escalation_autonomy`'s one-adjudication-pass-per-human-
touch bound (D18).

**Why it matters, and why it is small.** Bounded — the engagement it grants
logs the `enabler-examined` that retires the exemption, so the cost is one
Opus pass per closed escalation, not a loop, and the engagement still
verifies the closed issue's claim against reality before releasing anything
(`prompts/enabler.md`). But it is the same shape #934 named as out of scope
("a close that an examination **already** consumed and a *later* re-flag that
re-asks the settled question"), reachable through a door that check does not
watch — and the two exemptions it drags along are the ones the register
elsewhere treats as load-bearing.

**Why it was not fixed in #939.** The obvious tightening — also require that
no `unblocked` event has landed since the escalation — would break the very
case #939 exists to fix. In #706, the sharpest case, the block's own
`blocked`/`blocked:needs-refinement` labels came off at 2026-08-27T21:06Z,
between the human's close (20:30Z) and the Co-Ordinator's re-flag (23:18Z):
release-then-re-flag *is* the shape being rescued, so "no unblock since the
escalation" cannot be the guard. Distinguishing "released because the human's
close answered it" from "released long ago and now re-asked about something
else" needs a key this rule does not have today — plausibly the escalation's
own `blocked_ts` (`lib/enabler.sh` already records it, and #934 suggested
using it "where it sharpens the match") matched against the block lineage, or
the `decide-tactical` adjudication rung filed alongside #934, which is where
#934 put the neighbouring case.

**Where.**

- `lib/cycle-state.sh` — `ENABLER_ELIGIBLE_JQ`, `$examined_since_escalation`
  and the `issue-closed` branch.
- `lib/candidate-gather.sh`, `lib/candidate-select.sh` — the unblock paths
  that log no examination.
- `lib/refinement.sh` — `refinement_second_pass_refused`, the thrash guard
  the reason exempts.
- `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md` — requirement 35a.
