Requirement 17h (agent-ops#769, resolving agent-ops#844 option (b)) has the
Script compose `context`/`acceptance`/`title` itself, immediately before the
claim, for every source the fit ladder could ever trim (`issues`,
`tech-debt`) plus every other source the Script gathers as structured data —
never from a model's own paste. That closes the gap requirement 17g's
`item_text_fault`/`item_text_supply` (`lib/candidate-select.sh`) existed to
catch (a trimmed candidate's `acceptance` naming a specific detail its own
input never supported, #815/#821) — but it does more than close it: it
removes the condition those checks were scoped to ever seeing again.

`item_text_fault`/`item_text_supply` are gated on a candidate's `{repo,
item}` appearing in `coordinator_fit_trimmed_items`'s own output
(`lib/coordinator-input.sh`), which only ever names `issues`/`tech_debt`
entries — the fit ladder's own scope. Every candidate from either of those
two sources now reaches the claim loop already composed by requirement 17h
(`c_composed`, `agent-cycle.sh`'s per-candidate loop) — from a fresh live
read, never the trimmed extract these checks exist to check a model's paste
against — so the claim loop's own guard
(`(( selected_by_fallback || c_composed )) || c_fab_fault=…`) means
`item_text_fault` is called only for a candidate from one of the three
self-derived sources (`project-review`, `failed-runs`,
`implementation-plan`), none of which can ever appear in
`coordinator_fit_trimmed_items` (they have no pre-fetched band for the fit
ladder to trim in the first place). The scoping test at the top of
`item_text_fault` therefore always returns 0 with nothing to check; the
function, its `item_text_supply` counterpart, the `fab_faults` counter, and
the `cause: "fabricated"` stand-down branch are dead code, kept only as a
defence against a regression in requirement 17h's own compose step rather
than because either currently intercepts anything.

Removing them cleanly touches more than the two functions: `agent-cycle.sh`'s
claim loop (the two call sites and the `fab_faults` counter/stand-down
branch), `test/item-text-fabrication.test.sh` (which exists specifically to
exercise this now-unreachable path and would need retiring or rewriting
rather than left asserting behaviour that can no longer occur), and
`docs/IMPLEMENTATION-PIPELINE-SPEC.md`'s requirement 17g (which would need
rewriting as history rather than as a live requirement) plus its "Acceptance
checks" narrative (item 7h) and any dashboard vocabulary naming
`cause: "fabricated"`. That is real, well-understood, and bounded work, but
wider than the "small, reviewable" scope agent-ops#769's own pull request
kept to — noticed while implementing it (PR resolving agent-ops#769), and
deferred here rather than folded into that change.

Suggested fix: once requirement 17h has run in production for a while (long
enough to trust its own compose step is not itself regressing into the
fabrication/incompleteness failure modes 17g existed to catch), remove
`item_text_fault`/`item_text_supply`, their call sites, the `fab_faults`
counter and the `"fabricated"` stand-down cause, retire or rewrite
`test/item-text-fabrication.test.sh`, and rewrite requirement 17g as an
as-built account of why the check no longer exists rather than a still-live
requirement.
